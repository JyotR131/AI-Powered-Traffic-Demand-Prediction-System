# Traffic Congestion Demand Prediction - Approach Document

## Objective
The goal of this task was to design a system that predicts traffic demand in urban areas based on geographic, temporal, and environmental data. The performance metric is $100 \times R^2$ score.

## Data Analysis
- **Training Data**: 77,299 records spanning Day 48 and early parts of Day 49.
- **Test Data**: 41,778 records for Day 49 (different timestamps from the training data).
- **Missing Values**: Present in `RoadType`, `Temperature`, and `Weather`.
- **Temporal Patterns**: Demand shows strong correlation with `geohash` and `timestamp`. Comparing Day 48 and Day 49 (where both were available) showed a high correlation (~0.79), indicating strong daily patterns.

## Feature Engineering
1. **Time Features**: Converted the `timestamp` (H:M) into `hour`, `minute`, and `total_minutes`.
2. **Spatial Features**: Extracted geohash prefixes (lengths 3, 4, 5) to capture hierarchical spatial relationships.
3. **Target Encoding**:
   - Mean demand per `geohash`.
   - Mean demand per `hour`.
   - Mean demand per (`geohash`, `hour`) pair.
   These features were calculated using all available training data to capture the baseline demand for specific locations at specific times.

## Modeling
- **Algorithm**: LightGBM Regressor.
- **Validation Strategy**: Trained on Day 48 and validated on the Day 49 records available in the training set.
- **Hyperparameters**:
  - `learning_rate`: 0.01
  - `num_leaves`: 255
  - `feature_fraction`: 0.8
  - `bagging_fraction`: 0.8
- **Results**: Achieved an $R^2$ score of ~0.778 on the validation set.
